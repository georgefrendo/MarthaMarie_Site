Welcome. You may be feeling overwhelmed, caught in familiar patterns, or simply ready to understand yourself more deeply. There is room for all of that here.

My approach is collaborative and relational. We make space for your experience with curiosity, compassion, and respect for your own pace. I believe each person has an innate capacity for growth, and that meaningful change can begin with feeling heard.

With a PsyD background and a focus on Gestalt psychotherapy, I bring attention to the connections between your thoughts, emotions, body, and relationships. Together, we can explore what is happening now and discover new possibilities for how you want to live.

The Gestalt approach

More awareness.
More room for possibility.

Gestalt therapy invites you to notice your experience as it unfolds. Rather than searching for a way to “fix” yourself, we explore how you feel, connect, and respond—with space to try something new.

01 /

The Here & Now

Your past matters. We also notice how it lives in the present: a familiar thought, a feeling, or a pattern in your relationships. Awareness creates space to choose your next step.

02 /

Mind-Body Wholeness

Stress may show up as tight shoulders, shallow breathing, or a sense of restlessness. At your pace, we pay attention to these signals alongside your thoughts and emotions.

03 /

The Relational Space

Therapy is a partnership. An authentic, non-judgemental relationship offers space to feel understood, express what matters, and explore new ways of relating.

Beginning therapy can feel like a big step. Here are a few things you may be wondering.

Gestalt is a form of psychotherapy that considers your whole experience: thoughts, feelings, body, and relationships. Through conversation and optional experiential exercises, we explore what you notice in the present and how familiar patterns affect your life. You can always decline an exercise.

Session length, frequency, and fees will be discussed before you begin so that you know what to expect. Please ask about current appointment options during your consultation.

Please contact the practice to confirm current insurance participation, fees, and payment options. Coverage varies by plan; your insurer can explain your benefits and any reimbursement requirements.

We begin with what brings you to therapy, what you hope for, and any questions you have. We also discuss confidentiality and its limits, practical arrangements, and whether working together feels like a good fit. You choose how much to share.
